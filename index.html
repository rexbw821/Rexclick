Privacy policy

RexClick is a fully local application. Here is exactly what it does and doesn't do.

What we don't collect

  • We don't collect keystrokes or anything you type.
  • We don't send any data to any server.
  • We don't need an internet connection.
  • We don't track usage, analytics or behaviour.
  • We don't store any personal information.

What the app does

RexClick listens for keyboard events on your device only to choose which sound to play. Each key press is handled in memory and discarded straight away. Nothing is logged or saved. The app saves your settings (sound pack, volume, theme) on your own computer.

Keyboard access

RexClick uses a global keyboard hook, a standard operating-system feature also used by accessibility tools and keyboard remappers, to hear key presses in any app. It doesn't need administrator rights. On macOS, you allow it under Accessibility in System Settings.
